# Hi, I’m Devi👋
### Applied AI Engineer | Enterprise RAG, Agentic AI & Industrial Machine Learning

I build AI systems for complex enterprise and industrial workflows, combining LLM applications, machine learning and domain knowledge in energy and operations. I have 5+ years of experience across applied AI, production ML and data systems, and an MSc in Data Science (Energy Systems) from UCL.

Focus areas
- Agentic AI and LLM applications: LangGraph, LangChain, tool orchestration, structured outputs and human-in-the-loop workflows.
- Enterprise retrieval: RAG, document ingestion, hybrid retrieval, embeddings and vector databases.
- Industrial and energy AI: time-series anomaly detection, causal-risk analysis, renewable-energy analytics and power-system optimisation.
- ML engineering: Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, FastAPI and Docker.

Projects
- RL-UC Demand Responsiveness — compares Q-learning, DQN and PPO on a 51-generator unit-commitment benchmark and investigates demand responsiveness as a policy diagnostic.
- Wind Farm Curtailment — combines SCADA, weather, demand and market signals to study UK wind-farm curtailment.
- Time-to-Threat Survival Analysis — survival-analysis project for estimating time-to-threat in evacuation zones.
- Deep Learning for Smart Grid Monitoring — deep-learning work focused on smart-grid monitoring.

Technical toolkit
Python SQL PyTorch TensorFlow scikit-learn LangGraph LangChain FastAPI Docker PostgreSQL pgvector Milvus Langfuse

Background
